Mongle Playground Privacy Policy

Operator: zero vision · App: Mongle Playground (com.mongle.playground)
Document date: October 23, 2026

Scope

This policy describes the Android 1.0.0 closed-test preparation build with Google Play purchases disabled. We will update this page before public distribution or enabling purchases if data practices change.

Information kept on your device

The app does not require an app account or request a child's name, date of birth, email, location, contacts, photos, or microphone recordings. Language and volume settings and sketchbook drawings are stored locally in app storage. App code does not upload those settings or drawings. Clearing app data or uninstalling the app deletes local app data. Drawings are not guaranteed to return after reinstallation.

Audio, advertising, and external services

Pictures, recorded guidance, and sounds are bundled with the app. The app does not record audio or request voice generation from ElevenLabs during use. There are no ads or developer-operated behavioral analytics.

The app includes Google Play Billing Library 9.1.0 and its dependencies. Purchases are currently disabled, so the app does not create its billing client or connect to the purchase-verification server. Google independently processes information for services such as Play installation and updates under the Google Privacy Policy. Final review of included SDK diagnostics and network traffic in the Play-installed environment remains necessary before test distribution. This policy is not a verified declaration that the complete app collects no data.

Purchases are not active

The lifetime pass is being prepared and cannot currently be purchased. Sending purchase tokens to an operator verification server is not enabled. Before enabling payments, we will specify the purchase history, purchase token, random installation identifier, recipients, retention, and deletion procedures used for verification, restoration, refunds, and fraud prevention. The app will not directly request payment-card numbers or Google account passwords.

Children and parents

The primary audience is children aged 5 and under. Play screens and parent settings are separated by a parent check. Children are not asked to enter personal information or register on external websites. Parents can remove local settings and drawings by clearing the app's data on the device.

Contact and deletion requests

Support and privacy inquiries: zero535588@gmail.com. For parents only. The contact button opens a separate email app. It does not automatically send anything or attach device details, drawings, purchase tokens, or logs. If a parent chooses to send an email, the operator receives the sender's email address, the message they write, and any attachments they choose to include, for support and troubleshooting. Do not send children's names, dates of birth, contact details, or payment credentials. Email processing is subject to the email provider's services. We delete support email addresses, messages, and attachments within 90 days after the inquiry is resolved, unless retention is required by law, in which case the legally required retention period applies. Parents may request deletion of retained inquiries at the same address. Local settings and drawings can be removed by clearing app data on the device.

Visiting this website

This static page contains no advertising or analytics scripts. GitHub, the hosting provider, may process access information such as IP addresses to serve website requests. This is separate from local game data. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.
